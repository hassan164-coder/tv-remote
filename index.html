Connect to your Samsung TV

Your iPhone and TV must be on the same Wi‑Fi, and the TV must be on.

1
Enter the TV's IP address

On the TV: Settings → General (& Privacy) → Network → Network Status → IP Settings.

2
Trust the TV (once)

The TV uses its own security certificate. Tap below, then in Safari tap Show Details → visit this website → Visit Website. A blank or error page afterwards is normal. Come back here.

Open TV certificate page
3
Connect, then press “Allow” on the TV
Troubleshooting

• Stuck on “Connecting”: redo step 2, check the IP, check both are on the same Wi‑Fi (not a guest network).

• “Denied”: the TV blocked this remote. On the TV go to Settings → General → External Device Manager → Device Connection Manager → Device List, remove/allow “iPhone Remote”, then tap Forget pairing and connect again.

• Power ON usually won't work: when the TV is fully off its network is off too. Power OFF works.
